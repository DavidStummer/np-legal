News Poll Policies

Terms and Conditions

Effective 19 November 2026

These terms apply to News Poll, an independent app that runs on Reddit and lets people create and interact with polls about news articles. Your use of the app is also subject to Reddit’s rules and the rules of the community where you use it.

Creating polls

Submit public article links and content you have permission to share. Keep private links, passwords, and personal or sensitive information out of submissions. When you publish a poll, its source link, article preview, selected question, and any generated header image may appear in a Reddit post and be visible to people who can view that post.

Generated content

News Poll uses OpenAI to suggest poll questions, generate header illustrations, and review draft questions for moderation, spelling, grammar, and punctuation. Automated suggestions and moderation can make mistakes. Review the question and illustration before publishing. Generated illustrations may depict imagined scenes. See the Privacy Policy for details about what the app shares with service providers.

Acceptable use

Use News Poll lawfully. Do not use it to harass people, impersonate others, publish unlawful or infringing material, manipulate polling, bypass moderation, or disrupt the app. Polls may be removed under community or Reddit rules.

Availability and changes

Features may change or become unavailable. The current version displays demonstration vote totals and reports. Do not rely on them as measures of public opinion. If these terms change, the current version and its effective date will appear on this page.

Contact

For questions, reports, or requests about a poll, message the News Poll developer on Reddit.
