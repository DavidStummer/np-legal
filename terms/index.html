News Poll Policies

Terms and Conditions

Effective 23 November 2026

These terms apply to News Poll, an independent app that lets communities create, vote in, and share news polls. You must meet Reddit’s age and account requirements and follow applicable law, the Reddit User Agreement, Reddit Rules, and the rules of the community where you use the app.

Creating polls and sharing results

Submit public article links and content you have permission to share. Keep private links, passwords, access tokens, and personal or sensitive information out of submissions. When you confirm publication, the article source and preview, poll question, and any generated illustration can appear in a Reddit post. Confirming an image share creates a Reddit post under your account and News Poll adds a comment linking to the poll. A report can show your Reddit username as its creator. People who can view the post can see the content and username Reddit displays.

AI-generated content

News Poll uses OpenAI to draft or edit poll questions, generate illustrations, moderate wording, and draft share summaries. The app labels AI-generated or AI-edited content. Review generated wording, illustrations, and summaries before confirming publication. Automated suggestions and moderation can make mistakes. Illustrations are editorial and may depict imagined scenes. See the Privacy Policy for details about what the app sends to service providers.

Acceptable use and removals

Use News Poll lawfully. Do not use it to harass people, impersonate others, publish unlawful or infringing material, manipulate poll or Reddit votes, bypass moderation, or disrupt the app. Moderators and Reddit may remove content under their rules. Use Reddit’s report controls for posts and comments. Contact the developer to report app issues or request removal of information held by the app.

Availability and changes

Poll results reflect responses from participating accounts and may change as people vote. A shared image represents a snapshot at the time shown. News Poll polls are informal and are not representative measures of public opinion. App features depend on Reddit and external providers and may change or become unavailable.
