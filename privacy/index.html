News Poll Policies

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the independent News Poll developer processes information when you use the app on Reddit or visit these policy pages. You can contact the developer on Reddit.

Article previews and poll creation

When you request a preview, News Poll sends the public URL’s host and path to OpenAI to check whether it looks like an article. The app sends the URL to OpenGraph.io to retrieve the article’s title and description. Use public article URLs and keep account credentials, private links, access tokens, and personal or sensitive information out of the URL and draft question.

News Poll sends the article title and description to OpenAI to draft a poll question and an illustration. It sends your suggested wording for automated moderation and proofreading when you request a question review. To prepare a share preview, OpenAI receives the article title and description, poll question, and aggregate results for the selected segments. These requests exclude Reddit usernames, account IDs, and individual ballots. See OpenAI’s API data controls for details on provider processing and retention.

Information stored on Reddit

News Poll uses the Reddit account ID to associate your vote and saved voting location with you within the subreddit installation. When you vote, the app records your Yes or No selection and the country and optional US state you chose. It uses those selections to calculate geographic results and adjust counts when you change or withdraw a vote. Other users see aggregate results. In a region with few voters, the figures may reveal an individual response to someone who already knows who voted. Choosing a location also saves it for other News Poll posts in the same community.

News Poll stores poll wording, article URLs and previews, uploaded illustrations, post identifiers, creator details, and related-question indexes in Reddit-hosted storage. Saved reports contain their creator’s Reddit username, selected regions, and creation time. The creator’s username and poll or report contents can appear to people who can view them. A confirmed share creates an image post under the sharing account, with a snapshot of results and a link to the poll. News Poll uses the submitting account and image information briefly to identify asynchronously published image posts and attach the summary comment.

Retention

Saved locations stay in the subreddit installation until cleared or removed following a request. Individual votes can be withdrawn in the poll. Polls, reports, and results posts can remain available while they are hosted on Reddit. Article metadata is cached for up to seven days and article classification for up to 30 days. Generated illustrations may be reused for later polls about an article. Temporary share records expire after about ten minutes and creation preview and review records after about 30 minutes. Operational logs may retain diagnostic information on Reddit’s infrastructure. External providers apply their own retention policies and service settings.

Reddit handles platform data under the Reddit Privacy Policy. GitHub Pages hosts these policy pages. GitHub describes its handling of visitors’ IP addresses in its Privacy Statement.

Choices and requests

You can browse aggregate results without casting a vote, skip the optional state, clear your saved location, withdraw your vote, and cancel a share before posting. Use Reddit’s controls to delete a post or report a post or comment to moderators. For access, correction, deletion, restrictions, or other applicable privacy rights, or to report an app issue, message the News Poll developer. Give the poll link when relevant and keep sensitive information out of the message. Use Reddit’s privacy controls for information Reddit manages separately. Where applicable, you can also raise a concern with your data protection authority.
